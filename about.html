About the Creator

Chris Silva

Educator · Photographer · Designer · Builder
15+ YearsUS · Japan · MéxicoPhoto · Design · Web

I'm Chris Silva, an educator, photographer, designer, and builder with over 15 years of professional experience. I've worked across the United States, Japan, and México, capturing everything from weddings and portraits to commercial campaigns, motorsports, and the music industry. I co-own a photography and media production company with my wife Laura, who is also a photographer and educator.

My connection to image-making started with a single camera: a Nikon F3 my uncle Richard Martinez gave me before he passed. I still capture with it today, and I call it The Spirit Box, because every frame feels like a way to keep him with me. It reminds me that the work can honor memory, identity, and purpose, and that the people who shape us stay with us in what we make.

These days I spend as much time designing and building as I do behind the lens: brand and print work, and the websites and classroom apps you'll find here on SchoolFlo. I like making things that are clean, fast, and genuinely useful.

In Loving Memory

Recently, I lost my Tío Alex, Alejandro Lopez. He was a teacher at heart. He never needed a classroom to teach: everyone who came into contact with him walked away having learned something from him. He believed that no young person is beyond reach, that every one of them can be reached, and he lived that belief every day.

I carry his teaching spirit into my classroom, and I always will. Between Tío Alex and my uncle Richard Martinez, who first placed a camera in my hands, I am honored to carry both of their legacies with me every day I teach.
