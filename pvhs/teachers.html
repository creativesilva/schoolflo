Photography & Digital Arts

Chris Silva

Chris Silva is a fine art photographer and visual arts educator based in Santa Maria, California. He teaches Photography and Digital Arts at Pioneer Valley High School. Working mostly in black and white, across film, digital, and hybrid workflows, he makes documentary work, environmental portraits, and experimental images rooted in place and lived experience.

Capturing light since age 13 on 35mm film, Chris studied Applied Arts and Design at Allan Hancock College and earned a BA in Photography from Southern New Hampshire University. He co-founded Light Writerz, the PVHS photography club.

www.creativesilva.com

Photography

Laura Garcia

Laura Garcia is a professional photographer and photography educator at Pioneer Valley High School. She brings years of professional industry experience to the classroom.

Laura is a co-founder and advisor of Light Writerz, where she helps students build the discipline, technical skill, and creative confidence it takes to make photographs with intention.

www.chrisandlaura.com
