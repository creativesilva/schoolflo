Visual Arts · Fine Art

Fine
Art

Drawing, color, and painting by hand. Two courses that build observation skills, creative voice, and a body of finished artwork.

Foundation

Intro to Art

The elements and principles of art, drawing from observation, color, and mixed media. Open to all students.

Studio

Painting

Watercolor and acrylic, color mixing, composition, and a personal style, building toward finished paintings ready to exhibit.

Course details are being finalized with our Fine Art teacher and may change.

Level 1 · Foundation

Intro to Art

The foundation art course. Students learn the elements and principles of art and build core skills in drawing, color, and design by hand.

  • A-G creditUC / CSU “F” Visual and Performing Arts
  • Taught byStephanie Fikiri
  • Who it is forOpen to all students, no experience required. The starting point for fine art.
  • MaterialsGraphite, charcoal, ink, colored pencil, pastel, and paint, plus a personal sketchbook.
Sign up for Intro to Art

What students will be able to do

  • Use the elements and principles of art on purpose: line, shape, value, color, texture, space, balance, and emphasis.
  • Draw from observation using contour, value, proportion, and perspective.
  • Mix and apply color using color theory.
  • Work in a range of media, including collage, printmaking, and simple sculpture.
  • Describe, analyze, and give feedback on artwork using art vocabulary.
  • Keep a sketchbook to plan ideas and track growth.

Core units

Drawing fundamentalsLine, contour, value, shading, and proportion.
Color theoryThe color wheel, mixing, color schemes, and value scales.
Perspective & spaceOne and two point perspective, overlapping, and creating depth.
Mixed media & designCollage, printmaking, pattern, and an introduction to 3D work.
Art history & critiqueArtists across cultures and eras, and how to talk about art.

Example projects

  • Contour Line Drawing
  • Value Scale and Shaded Sphere
  • Color Wheel
  • One Point Perspective
  • Still Life Drawing
  • Self-Portrait Drawing
  • Collage
  • Printmaking

How the year builds

Start with observation and drawing basics, add color, then explore mixed media. The year closes with a finished piece ready to exhibit, which sets up Painting.

Level 2 · Studio

Painting

The studio painting course. Students explore watercolor and acrylic, color mixing, and composition, and develop a personal style.

  • A-G creditUC / CSU “F” Visual and Performing Arts
  • Taught byStephanie Fikiri
  • Who it is forStudents who completed Intro to Art.
  • MaterialsWatercolor, acrylic, brushes, palette knives, watercolor paper, and canvas.
Sign up for Painting

What students will be able to do

  • Mix color with control over value, temperature, and intensity.
  • Use painting techniques in watercolor and acrylic: washes, layering, blending, dry brush, and texture.
  • Plan compositions with thumbnail sketches and studies.
  • Paint from observation and imagination: still life, landscape, portrait, and abstraction.
  • Study painters and art movements, and respond to them in their own work.
  • Build a series and present finished paintings for exhibit.

Core units

Color & mixingColor charts, value, temperature, and limited palettes.
WatercolorWashes, wet on wet, layering, and lifting.
AcrylicBlending, layering, texture, and painting on canvas.
Composition & subjectStill life, landscape, portrait, and abstraction.
Painters & movementsMaster studies and art history connections.
Series & exhibitionA cohesive body of work, artist statements, and display.

Example projects

  • Color Mixing Chart
  • Watercolor Landscape
  • Monochromatic Painting
  • Still Life in Acrylic
  • Master Study
  • Self-Portrait Painting
  • Abstract Painting
  • Series for Exhibit

Studio rhythm

Every painting moves through the same steps: thumbnails, a color study, the final painting, and a short critique or reflection.
