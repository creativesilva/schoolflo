Visual Arts · Digital Media

Digital
Media

A Career Technical Education program in the Design, Visual, and Media Arts pathway. Two pathways, Digital Photography and Digital Arts, built on real cameras and industry software.

Two Pathways

Start with level 1, grow into level 2

Digital Media

Digital Photography

  • Photography 1Foundation · no experience needed
  • Photography 2Advanced · lighting and studio

Operate a real camera on Manual, see like a photographer, shape light on purpose, and finish images in Adobe Lightroom Classic and Photoshop.

Explore PhotographySign up

Digital Media

Digital Arts

  • Digital Arts 1Foundation · no experience needed
  • Digital Arts 2Advanced · brand and portfolio

Learn the elements and principles of design, then use Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop to make clean, intentional graphics, brands, and portfolio work.

Explore Digital ArtsSign up
  • Bilingual materials. In Digital Media, project directions and handouts are offered in English and Spanish, and students may submit their work in either language.
  • Fine arts credit. Every Visual Arts course satisfies the UC and CSU A-G “F” Visual and Performing Arts requirement.
  • Career: a CTE pathway in Arts, Media, and Entertainment, with SkillsUSA.
