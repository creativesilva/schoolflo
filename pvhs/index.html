About the program

Hands on, not lectures

PVHS Visual Arts brings together two areas: Digital Media, a Career Technical Education program in the Design, Visual, and Media Arts pathway, and Fine Art, where students build drawing and painting skills by hand.

In every class, students learn by making: real tools, clear steps, and finished work they can present. By the end of a course sequence, students have a body of work that shows what they can do.

  • Two areas, one program. Digital Media covers Digital Photography and Digital Arts. Fine Art covers Intro to Art and Painting.
  • Bilingual materials. In Digital Media, project directions and handouts are offered in English and Spanish, and students may submit their work in either language.
  • Fine arts credit. Every Visual Arts course satisfies the UC and CSU a-g “F” Visual and Performing Arts requirement.
  • Open to everyone. Photography 1, Digital Arts 1, and Intro to Art need no experience.
UC / CSU a-gFVisual & Performing Arts

College ready elective

The “F” in a-g

Every Visual Arts course, from Photography and Digital Arts to Intro to Art and Painting, satisfies the UC and CSU a-g “F” Visual and Performing Arts requirement.

To be eligible for the University of California and California State University, students need a year of Visual and Performing Arts. Any Visual Arts course covers that requirement, and Digital Media also builds real career skills on industry tools, so one elective can work toward college and career at the same time.

  • College: counts as fine art (“F”) for UC and CSU admission.
  • Career: a CTE pathway in Arts, Media, and Entertainment, with SkillsUSA.

Career Technical Student Organization

SkillsUSA

The PVHS Digital Media program is associated with SkillsUSA for both the Digital Photography and Digital Arts pathways.

SkillsUSA is a national organization for career and technical students. It helps students build leadership, teamwork, and workplace skills, and gives them a place to show their technical work beyond the classroom.

Pathway status

Concentrator

A student who builds depth in one pathway by moving past the first course, for example Photography 1 into Photography 2, or Digital Arts 1 into Digital Arts 2.

Pathway status

Completer

A student who finishes the full course sequence in a pathway. Completers leave with a body of finished work and real industry skills.

How Every Project Runs

The same five steps in every Digital Media course
  1. Learn the conceptA short, plain language overview introduces the idea and its key vocabulary.
  2. Make or capturePhotograph with a camera kit, or build a design in Adobe software.
  3. Edit and finishCull, edit, and export the work to a professional standard.
  4. Turn it inEach step submits a clear deliverable to Canvas: a photo, a contact sheet, a design file, a high resolution export.
  5. ReflectA short written reflection: what I did, what I learned, what I would change.

Students are graded on following the process and meeting the standard for each deliverable, not on being a “natural.” Clear, repeatable expectations are the point.
