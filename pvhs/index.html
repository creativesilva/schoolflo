About the program

Hands on, not lectures

Digital Media at Pioneer Valley is a Career Technical Education program in the Arts, Media, and Entertainment sector, inside the Design, Visual, and Media Arts pathway.

Students learn industry standard tools and professional habits through hands on projects. By the end of the pathway, a student can operate real equipment, run a professional edit and deliver workflow, and present finished creative work.

  • A bilingual classroom. Every project and handout is offered in English and Spanish. Students may work and submit in either language.
  • A Mac lab. Students work on Macs, with a school iPad that doubles as a scanner for sketches and hand done work.
  • Open to everyone. Photography 1 and Digital Arts 1 need no experience.

How Every Project Runs

The same five steps in every course
  1. Learn the conceptA short, plain language overview introduces the idea and its key vocabulary.
  2. Make or capturePhotograph with a camera kit, or build a design in Adobe software.
  3. Edit and finishCull, edit, and export the work to a professional standard.
  4. Turn it inEach step submits a clear deliverable to Canvas: a photo, a contact sheet, a design file, a high resolution export.
  5. ReflectA short written reflection: what I did, what I learned, what I would change.

Students are graded on following the process and meeting the standard for each deliverable, not on being a “natural.” Clear, repeatable expectations are the point.
